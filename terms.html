1. About XDesk

XDesk is a remote access program for Windows 10 and 11, sold by subscription on the Microsoft Store. It lets you reach computers over the internet or the local network. XDesk has no account or sign-up of its own, shows no ads and collects no data for third parties.

Connections over the internet use the developer's own server (ID and relay server), located in Brazil and reached at the address xdesk.ddns.net. No session data passes through third-party servers.

1.1 Roles

  • Developer and controller: Jeferson Y Castro de Oliveira, an individual, who develops and sells XDesk and operates the server, and decides how data is processed.
  • User: whoever uses the program to access a computer or to be accessed.
  • Subscriber: a user who pays the subscription to access other computers.
  • Microsoft: sells XDesk through the Microsoft Store and processes payment, under its own privacy policy.
  • Professional use: whoever uses XDesk to serve clients (for example, a technician) is the controller of those clients' data.

1.2 Contact and data protection officer

Individuals and small-scale processing agents are exempt from appointing a data protection officer (ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 2/2022). Even so, there is a channel for privacy and support requests: kodego@hotmail.com. Privacy requests are answered within 15 days, and support requests within 5 business days.
